# Field & Veld

An offline farm record book for Windows and Android. It keeps records of your
fields, crops, plantings, spraying and weeding, scouting reports, harvests,
sales, costs and crew — all on your own computer or phone.

- **No internet needed** — not to install it, not to use it
- **No account to sign up for, nothing to pay**
- **Your records stay on your device** — encrypted, nothing is sent anywhere

---

## Download

**Get the latest build from the
[Releases page](https://github.com/tatendachitima/field-and-veld/releases).**

| | File | Size |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** 10/11, 64-bit | `Field-Veld-0.9.1-x64-setup.exe` | ~213 MB |
| **Android** phone or tablet | `field-and-veld-0.9.1-arm64.apk` | ~40 MB |

Both are large-ish because everything they need is packed inside. Nothing is
fetched while installing, so they work the same on a farm with no signal as in
town — copy them over on a flash drive or by cable.

> **Windows will warn you the first time.** You'll see a blue
> *"Windows protected your PC"* box. Click **More info → Run anyway**. This is
> expected for any program that hasn't been through Microsoft's paid signing
> process. Full details in [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).

> **Android will warn you too.** Sideloaded apps need "install unknown apps"
> allowed for whichever app you open the file with. Details in
> [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md).

## Quick start

**Windows**

1. Download `Field-Veld-0.9.1-x64-setup.exe` from the Releases page.
2. Double-click it. When Windows warns you, click **More info → Run anyway**.
3. Open **Field & Veld** from the Start menu and set a password.

**Android**

1. Download `field-and-veld-0.9.1-arm64.apk` onto the phone.
2. Open it and allow installing from that app when asked.
3. Open **Field & Veld** and set a password.

At the end of the short tour, take the offer to **load the sample farm** — a
season of fields, plantings and activity you can prod without worrying about
breaking anything. Settings → Data & sync clears it out when you're ready to
enter your own records.

Complete instructions — backing up, the worker portal, voice messages — are in
**[INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)**.

## What it does

- **Fields & crops** — your blocks of land and what you grow (ready-made Zimbabwean crop templates)
- **Plantings** — a crop, in a field, on a date (the record everything else hangs off)
- **Activities, scouting, harvests** — day-to-day field work
- **Sales, costs, inventory** — money in and out
- **Labour** — your crew, with PINs for the worker portal
- **Reports** — profit & loss, budgets, meal planning
- **Worker portal** — phones on the same wifi log scouting and harvests with picture-based forms

## What's new in 0.9.1

- **Android build.** The same app, laid out for a phone: a bottom bar for the
  things you open it to do, everything else one tap away, and lists that read
  as cards instead of a spreadsheet squeezed sideways.
- Settings collapsed into sections you open as needed.
- The plantings timeline no longer hides itself behind the planting name.
- Sample crops and the crop templates now come with pictures.

**Known gaps on Android**, being worked on: voice notes are recorded but not
written out to text on the phone (they still are on Windows), and backing up
or exporting to a file is not wired to Android's file picker yet. Everything
else — records, reports, the worker portal — works on both.
